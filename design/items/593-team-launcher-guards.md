The team launcher refuses to overspend or double-launch (#593; no schema change; 2026-11-20). `proto/scripts/team start` gains two checks that run before any credential is issued. TEAM_MAX_TOTAL_USD, when set, is a team-wide dollar cap: start adds the cap of every worker it would launch (TEAM_MAX_BUDGET each) to what the workers already in TEAM_DIR have spent (the cost their log recorded; a still-running worker counts at its full cap) and refuses the whole launch with exit 2 when the sum passes the cap, or when the value is not a number. The ticket check reads the board as the launcher (`claims` in TEAM_ROOM) and does not launch a task whose `ticket #N` is held by another member; that task is reported and skipped (exit 1) while the others go on. A claim held by the worker's own name does not block a relaunch, and a board that cannot be read warns and launches anyway, since the room may not exist yet.

The third gap in #593, relaunching a worker that exited with unread mail, is not built: the launcher cannot see unread mail for a revoked member, and relaunching spends money, so it needs a human decision (options in #1011).
