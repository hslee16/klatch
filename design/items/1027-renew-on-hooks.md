A credential renews on the hooks that prove a session is alive (#1027; no schema change; 2026-11-21). A worker's credential idles out after its idle timeout, and the session-end hook could then no longer mark the member done (set_status is guarded), so it aged off the board. The human chose to make idle expiry rarer rather than to widen what a dead credential may do. The hook methods that prove the session is alive, `sign_of_life` (every hook but Stop and SessionEnd) and `record_hook_run` for any event but `SessionEnd` (so Stop too), now renew the credential as a successful request does: the expiry moves to now plus the idle timeout, never past the cap from issue, throttled, and audited as `renew-credential` with the new expiry and never the token.

    Only a live credential renews: a hook after the expiry or a revoke is refused as before and brings nothing back. `SessionEnd` renews nothing, and a session end after an idle gap longer than the limit behaves as before (claims freed, hook run recorded, credential ended; the status is not set). No other own-record method renews, so a request from outside the hooks keeps nothing alive. Proof is in `credential_renewal.rs`.
