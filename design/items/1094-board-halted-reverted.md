The board shows the integrator's reverted and halted words (#1094, from #867 of epic #863; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
The integrator (#866, #867) posts fixed words when it reverts a merge or stops: `reverted: #N`, `halted: main red after #N` and `halted: main not verified after #N` (exit code 5). Until now they were plain lobby lines, so `klatch board` still showed the PR as merged and nothing on the board said the integrator had stopped. Decision: fold them in the same place and by the same rules as the queue words of item 864, derived from the log, stored nowhere, nothing read from the code host.

Words. `reverted: #N` from an integrator or the human moves a queue entry that is shown as merged to a new state `reverted`, shown as `#N reverted, main went red (3m ago)`. From anyone else, for a PR that is not queued, or for a PR in any other state, it is ignored, as the other state words are. A reverted PR is not enqueued again by `ready:` and a later `merged: #N` does not undo it (a fixed PR is a new PR); a plain member's `merged: #N` still takes the entry out of the queue, as item 864 says. `halted: main red after #N` and `halted: main not verified after #N` from an integrator or the human make one halt (`merge_halt` in `domain/merge_queue.rs`): the newest one counts, and it ends when a later state word from an integrator or the human (`integrating:`, `checking:`, `merged:`, `dropped:`, `reverted:`) shows the integrator is running again. A `ready:` word does not end it, because anyone can post one. Any other text after `halted:` is ignored, so nothing the integrator posts is echoed except the number.

Where it shows. The first line of the `queue` section of `klatch board`: `integrator halted: main red after #7 (3m ago; it merges nothing more until a human looks at main and starts it again)`. A halt alone makes the section appear, with the usual heading that says who holds `merge:main`. The board field is `halt`; there is no new command, wire method or MCP tool. The three words are rows in CONVENTIONS.md and `CONVENTION_WORDS`, and `QUEUE_WORDS` gains `reverted:` and `halted:` so the store reads them by prefix at any depth.

What this does not do. Nothing verifies a halt or a revert: the board shows what an integrator or the human said, as item 864 says for the other words. A halt stays shown until an integrator word follows it, so a human who clears the cause and starts the integrator sees it go when the integrator posts its next state word, not when the process starts. The role is read when the board is built, so removing the role hides its halt.

Tests: `domain/merge_queue/tests.rs` (reverted from the integrator, from a member, for an unqueued PR, not re-enqueued; the halt words with both reasons, ignored forms, newest wins, ended by each state word and not by a member's), `gateway/tests/merge_queue.rs` (the board through the gateway with roles, the section text, a halt with no entries) and the convention word rows (`the_code_reads_every_example_as_its_word`, `conventions_doc.rs`).
