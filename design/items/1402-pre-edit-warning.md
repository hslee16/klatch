Pre-edit overlap warning and first-edit passive claim (#1402, #1458, epic #1400; no schema or wire change; 2026-11-27). A PreToolUse step that tells an agent, before an Edit, Write, MultiEdit or NotebookEdit, that another member holds the path, and (second consent) takes the passive claim before the first edit.

    Decisions. (1) One PreToolUse entry of its own with `--pre-edit-warn` (own matcher, edit tools only), separate from the blocking `--enforce` entry: `is_enforce` is "ours and not pre-edit", so each consent is installed, refreshed and removed without touching the other, and `HookKind::PreEdit` lets doctor and `klatch consent` tell them apart. Without the flag the event is the blocking hook exactly as before; nothing in toolguard changed except one visibility change. (2) The claim before the first edit (#1458) is its own word, `--pre-edit-claims`, which implies the warning and requires `--passive-claims`: the claim is released by the Stop and SessionEnd hooks that flag installs, so without it the claim would only lapse after 300 seconds. Turning passive claims off drops the first-edit claim and keeps the warning. It complements the PostToolUse step (renewals stay there) and replaces nothing. (3) The look-up is read only and uses the guarded `claims` call, so a room the member may not read contributes nothing, the same scope test as the blocking hook applies, and no message is read or consumed. A claim is taken only when nobody else holds the path, so a warning records no claim wait. (4) The line names holders (three, then a count), the claim count and the longest time left, never a reason or message text, and is cut and stripped of control characters like every hook line; a passive claim path is read from the repository root and an explicit one from the working directory.

    Limit, stated honestly. There is no record of an edit apart from a claim, so "the first member edited it a minute ago" is warned about only while its claim is live (an explicit claim, or a passive one until its turn ends). A turn that ended released its passive claim, and the next member's edit is then not warned about. Closing that needs a small edit record (path, member, time) with its own retention and privacy decision, which is a schema change and is left to the owner; it is ticketed. A subagent's tool call (its input carries `agent_id`) is left alone, as the passive claims step does.
