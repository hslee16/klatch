Refused-room notes are bounded by the hook budget (#612; no schema change; 2026-11-19).
A session start prints one note per room the guard would not show. They were subtracted from the hook budget but never capped, so enough refused rooms could shrink the digest to nothing and then push the whole hook text past `HOOK_MAX_BYTES`, where Claude Code keeps only a 2 KB preview. The notes now share a fixed budget (`REFUSED_MAX_BYTES`, 600 bytes, trailer included) and are cut by whole lines, ending with "[klatch] N more refused rooms not shown]". The same bound applies to the digest of every other hook.
