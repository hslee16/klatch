Decision by the human, option A: no change to key custody. The label keys a process holds follow the member's global clearance (`sealed_labels_within`); a room-scoped grant (#893) raises clearance for that room's checks only. A grantee in a room whose label is sealed would therefore pass the clearance check and then fail closed on every read and post. `grant_room_clearance` now refuses at grant time with rule `sealed-key` when the room's label is sealed and the grantee's global clearance does not reach it, and the message names the next step (`klatch admin clear <member> <label>`, then grant again, or leave the room ungranted). The check runs inside the same store transaction as the other grant rules and a refusal keeps its audit row. A grantee that already holds the global clearance, and any room whose label is not sealed, are unchanged. The human's `klatch admin room-clear` is a floor act and is not checked.
