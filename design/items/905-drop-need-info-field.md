The facilitator need-info DM loses its optional field (#905, #997; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
The need-info DM of #902 was `need-info #N: CODE` or `need-info #N: CODE (FIELD)`, with a free field of at most 40 characters guarded by a check that it did not repeat another member's lobby text. The check could not see claim notes, statuses or other rooms, could not catch re-worded text, and scanned only `tail(lobby, 0, 5000)`, the oldest 5000 lobby messages (#905, #997, the half of #980 that stayed open). The human decided on 2026-11-21 to drop the field rather than extend the check. The DM is now exactly `need-info #N: CODE`, a code from the fixed set of five and nothing else, so no text derived from any member can ride in it and the scan is gone.

    Removed: the field in `parse_need_info` (it now returns the ask number and the code), `NEED_INFO_FIELD_MAX`, the repeat scan in `check_need_info`, the field wording in the facilitator brief and its brief test, the field rows of `proto/AUTHZ-AUDIT.md` (the known-gaps row is now a fixed row) and the field text of the #902 item. The one-need-info-per-ask rule (#980, by `words`) and the role bound (the exact form only, under the rule `facilitator`) are unchanged. The ask flow itself is untouched: a helper's `need-info #N: <question>` and the asker's `info #N:` are ordinary convention words (`parse_clarification`), and the facilitator code form is one narrow case of the first.

    A message that carries the old text, `need-info #1: which-file (login form)`, is an ordinary message: any member may post it, it is no longer the DM form, and a facilitator posting it is refused by the role bound like any other free text. Proof: `a_message_with_the_old_need_info_field_text_is_an_ordinary_message` and the reworked malformed-DM cases in `gateway/tests/facilitator.rs`, and the wire tests in `session/facilitator_tests.rs`. The tests were written first and watched fail.
