The ask supervisor can charge a measured cost per helper (#1177, follow-up to #1170; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
`proto/scripts/ask-supervisor` charged a fixed `ASKSUP_START_USD` per helper start, so the total spend cap was only a count of starts. Every helper now gets `ASKSUP_COST_FILE` (a file in the supervisor's state directory) in its environment. When the helper ends the supervisor reads it: if the first 32 bytes are exactly one decimal number (USD, a trailing newline allowed) that number replaces the fixed charge made at the start, capped at `ASKSUP_MAX_TOTAL_USD`, so one runaway report cannot do more than fill the cap. A missing or empty file, a second line, any other text, a negative or exponent number, or a value over 24 characters is ignored and the fixed charge stays. The content is never run and never printed. A helper stopped by a signal is not settled (the fixed charge stays; the supervisor is ending anyway). Tests use the fake launcher: replacement, higher cost, absent, eight kinds of junk, trailing text, the cap on one report, and the total reaching the cap stopping further starts.
