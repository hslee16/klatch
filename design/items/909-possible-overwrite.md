A claim taken on a path just released after a deadlock yield is flagged as a possible_overwrite stuck event (#909, option C, human decision 2026-11-21; schema 35 to 36; 2026-11-21).
`klatch stuck ROOM` and the wire method `stuck` gain a seventh kind, `possible_overwrite` (`room`, `path`, `previous`, `new`; key `possible_overwrite:ROOM:PATH:PREVIOUS:NEW`). **What it flags:** the overwrite after a deadlock of #272: both parties to a claim deadlock, one lets go of a path and the other then takes it, so the earlier work may be replaced. It flags the pattern and never judges whether the second edit built on the first (that is semantic); it is a prompt to check, not proof. **What it needs:** claim history was not enough (a release deletes the claim row and a refusal leaves only a wait that the take then clears), so one small table, `claim_yields` (migration 35 to 36), keeps names, paths and times and never text. A refusal that closes a cycle marks its requester and every holder on the cycle; a member so marked that releases a live claim (`klatch release`, with or without a note) records the release; a claim taken by another member on an overlapping path, while the release is within the window, names that member as the taker. **Bounded window:** `YIELD_WINDOW_SECS` is 600, fixed and not a setting. A release counts only within 600 s of the deadlock mark, a take only within 600 s of the release, and the event is shown for 600 s after the take; rows older than that are deleted on the next claim attempt. **Not flagged:** a release with no deadlock first (a plain hand over), a plain conflict that is not a cycle, a member outside the cycle, the same member taking its own path again, and a claim that lapses, is released by a session end or by anchor instead of by `release`, which are not recorded. **The line:** the mediator proposes with one fixed template from the event's fields only, `mediator proposal: @NEW took PATH in ROOM after @PREVIOUS released it following a claim deadlock. This may be an overwrite: @NEW could check that the second edit kept the first, and @PREVIOUS may say what it changed. A proposal only: ...`; `PATH` is the claim key when it is plain and `(unnamed)` otherwise (the form of #1182), names are validated like every mediator line, and a case naming the mediator is skipped. **Fixtures:** `overwrite-after-deadlock` in `fixtures/stuck-cases.json` now has `detectable: true`, one `possible_overwrite` event and one proposal; the design item for #271 is updated. **Decision recorded:** the other case, two tasks that need one result (differing wording, or a shared result with no shared path), stays with the model-backed mediator (#898, #190); rules compare paths and numbers, never wording, and no produces/needs field was added (option B was not chosen).
