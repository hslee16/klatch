Claude Sonnet is the recommended hosted judge (#942; no schema change, no code change; 2026-11-19).
The judge evaluation (proto/klatch-bench/judge-eval/results/2026-11-19) scored five judges on two corpora, 601 scored items with contested items excluded: the rules judge, Jev (TypeSafe), Claude Sonnet, Claude Opus and Claude Fable 5.2. Sonnet is the decision: 99.5 and 100.0 percent on corpus 1 (dev, test) and 88.8 and 87.3 percent on the hard corpus, with 0 to 1 data leaks and the lowest cost of the three Claude models. Opus is lower on both corpora and costs 1.7 to 2.9 times as much; Fable 5.2, the strongest model available and the blind labelling check (it differed from the stored labels on four items, three of which stand), matches Sonnet on the hard corpus, is below it on corpus 1, and costs 1.6 to 4 times as much. Jev alone over-blocks 18 percent of clean hard items and leaks 28 to 34 percent, and the rules judge misses most of the hard items, so neither is a judge on its own.

What the decision means. When an operator turns on a hosted judge, the supported choice is a Claude Sonnet reviewer, quarantined as in D4 (one candidate message plus the rules, no tools, a fixed schema back). Nothing changes by default: the hosted judge stays off, it sees only text posted strictly below the configured ceiling label (#872), and a missing label, an unknown ceiling or any transport error defers. The `CascadeJudge` (#936) and the Jev client (#898) stay as opt-in tooling: the cascade saves 3 to 26 percent of Sonnet calls on hard traffic (18 to 41 percent on easy traffic) and its low deny thresholds start to over-block, so it is not the recommended configuration. The bench judge (`klatch-bench judge-eval --judge claude:sonnet`) is the reference for what a Sonnet judge does with the rules in its prompt.

Limits. One author's labels and one labelling check (#924): an independent human labeller is still open. Figures are single runs. Redactions that keep a person's name beside removed data are the main leak and a stronger model did not close it, so it is a rules and labelling question. `hinjl-009` was relabelled from allow to deny by the owner on 2026-11-19 after all three Claude models denied it, so earlier result files score it against the old label. Re-run the evaluation before changing the recommendation, and when a new model generation ships.
