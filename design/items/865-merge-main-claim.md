The merge:main claim serializes merging: who may take it, its lease and its park bound (#865, epic #863; no schema change; 2026-11-20). One integrator merges at a time, and the way klatch says so is an ordinary claim named `merge:main`, so the board, `claims`, the wait hook and the claim age reminders already show who holds it. Nothing in the klatch binary talks to GitHub or holds a credential; the integrator (#866) does the code host work with its own login and only takes and releases this claim here.

Who may take it. A new role, `integrator`, granted by the human at a terminal (`klatch admin integrator <member>`, `--off` to remove it), stored like the other roles in `role:<member>` (one role per member, audited as `integrator`). It is an ordinary member in every other way and gets no admin act. The gateway refuses the claim (and the lease question `claim_ttl_for` that the command asks first) for anyone else with the rule `integrator`, naming the next step (ask the human to run the grant): a plain member, the admin agent, the librarian, reviewer and facilitator. The human's own name (`admin`, which no typed name, credential or session can be on a members' gateway, #523) is allowed. Any spelling of the name (`Merge:Main`, with spaces) is the same claim, so the rule cannot be walked around and the queue (#864) reads one name. The claim is shared across scopes: a second integrator in another repository scope is still refused, and the refusal names the holder like any claim conflict.

The lease. The default lease is the usual 10 minutes and a request is capped at 30 minutes (`MERGE_CLAIM_MAX_SECS`, capped as an ask is, not refused). A holder that is active renews it for the lease it was made with, as any claim; one that goes silent lapses at the end of its lease and the next integrator may take it. The liveness probe never stretches it: other claims of a holder the probe sees waiting on a tool are held for as long as the probe says, but the #533 lesson (a claim held for an hour while its owner waited) is that a waiting holder is not working, so `merge:main` is left out of the stretch. The probe may still shorten it (a holder found gone or stuck).

The park bound. A `parked: <why>, back by <time>` post stretches every other claim of the member to the return time, up to a week. For `merge:main` the stretch stops at 30 minutes from the park (`MERGE_PARK_MAX_SECS`), whatever return time was said, and a park never shortens it. This is what an event-driven integrator needs: it parks while it waits for a check suite event, and if the event forwarder dies nothing wakes it, it stays silent, and the claim lapses at the bound instead of blocking merges for hours. A holder that wakes and posts gets a normal lease back (the unpark rule). Release is the holder's `release`, or the claims of a member are cut by the usual rules (a removed member, `anchor-done`).

Limits. The claim is per room like every claim, so the team takes it in one room (the lobby); a second room would be a second claim. An active holder renews indefinitely, so the age reminders at 2 hours remain the only check on a holder that is busy but not merging. Both are recorded in the proof tests in `gateway/tests/merge_claim.rs` and `store/sqlite/leases.rs`.
