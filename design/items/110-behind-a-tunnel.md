Reaching a loopback-only klatch-server through a tunnel, and every HTTP route in the audit (#110, #161, epics #13 and #152; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
Part of #110 is built without a new listener or a trust decision: the deployment guide `clients/recipes/behind-a-tunnel.md` (an ssh local forward to the loopback listener, a same-host mutual-TLS terminator, and the forced-command transport of #107 when a port is not needed) and the tests that pin the one rule every pattern relies on: identity is the worker credential and nothing a proxy adds. The listener stays loopback only and `bind_loopback` is untouched; no off-loopback bind is possible, and the refusal text still names #157 and #161.

**Tests.** `forwarded_identity_headers_give_no_identity_and_change_none` (`klatch-server/tests/http_wire.rs`): every route is `401` with forwarded-address, forwarded-user and client-certificate headers and no credential (from a loopback source, as a same-host proxy would be), and a credential for `w-1` with headers naming `w-2` is a `w-1` session. `no_source_reads_a_forwarded_header_or_trusts_a_peer_address` (`klatch-server/tests/route_audit.rs`) scans every source file for those header names and `peer_addr`. `every_route_is_named_by_a_row_of_the_authorization_audit` finds each route constant and route arm of the crate and fails when no audit row names it; it was red first because the webhook intake route `/github` had no row (now `proto/audit/06a-klatch-server-webhook-intake/`). The guide has `clients/recipes/test_behind_a_tunnel_doc.py` (loopback-only commands, the identity and ignored-header statements, the same-host rule for a plaintext hop, `not run` for ssh and stunnel, links resolve).

**Not built, by design.** Forwarded identity accepted from configured proxy addresses and audited when ignored (the rest of #110's done-when) would turn a header into an identity; it waits for a threat-model decision recorded on the ticket. Native TLS and client certificates (#108, #109, #157), the WebSocket and gRPC connectors (#112, #113) and remote exposure (#300, #1061) are blocked on the TLS library, certificate management and enrollment decisions, also on their tickets. The ssh and stunnel lines in the guide were not run.
