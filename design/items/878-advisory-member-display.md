A flag-mode member shows as advisory in who and the board (#878, follows #61; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
A room-require in `flag` mode admits a joiner whose harness lacks a required capability and records `harness-flagged` in the refusals log, but nothing a reader sees said so. **Shape:** the admission writes the policy key `room:ROOM:advisory:MEMBER` (value `flag`) when it flags, and removes it when the same member is next admitted cleanly or the room has no requirement, so the latest admission decides. `Gateway::with_advisory` fills `MemberInfo.advisory` for `who` and the board from one prefix read; the text is `advisory: harness lacks a capability this room asked for` in `who` and `, advisory (...)` in the board line. **Why a policy key:** no schema change, and it stays out of the refusals log, which is a bounded evidence record that may be pruned. **Limits:** the mark is display only and never authority (the adapter that declared the harness is an untrusted conveyor, so it says what the harness claims, not what is true), it names no capability, and a requirement lifted after a flagged join leaves the mark until that member rejoins. Marking the sender on `read` and digest message lines is not done here (it needs the mark on every message the store returns); it is tracked separately.
