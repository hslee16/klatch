Real headless runs on 2026-11-21 (#210, #239, #241, #1146 comment) showed agents never used `dm`, `find`, `ask`/`answer`, `reopen` or the outcome words unless the prompt named them, and moved to a dm only when told (variant C). So the text is what decides adoption.
