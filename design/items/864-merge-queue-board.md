The merge queue on the board: `ready: #N` enqueues, the integrator's words say how it goes, all derived from words and claims (#864, step 2 of epic #863; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
The slow part of building with klatch was merging, and #865 gave the team one claim, `merge:main`, that serializes it. This item gives the queue a place to be seen. Decision: derive it exactly as item 84 derives the open-PR section (words in the log, claims for who holds what, nothing stored, nothing read from the code host), so the binary stays network-free and credential-free and the integrator (#866) only posts fixed words from its own `gh webhook forward` events.

Words. `ready: #N` enqueues PR N (`N`, `#N` and `PR N` are read, as for `pr:`, and text after the number is ignored). The integrator posts the state: `integrating: #N` (it is merging this PR now), `checking: #N` (the checks it waits for are running), `merged: #N` (the existing item 84 word) and `dropped: #N <reason>`. The reason is the first word after the number and comes from a fixed list: `conflict` (shown as a code conflict with main), `checks-failed`, `closed`, `reverted` (merged and then reverted because main went red, #867); any other word shows as `other reason`, so the integrator's text is never echoed. The words are in `QUEUE_WORDS` next to the fold and are read by the `MessageStore::words` prefix query (item 85), not from a window of the oldest messages.

Who may post. `ready:` is any member's, for any PR, not only its owner: a PR number is a name and not a verified PR (item 84 says so), the claim `pr:N` is held by whoever took it and is unverified, and the one that says a PR is ready is as often a reviewer or the facilitator as the author; `ready:` only asks, and the integrator checks the facts before it merges. A second `ready:` for a queued PR is idempotent and keeps its place and its first sender. The state words count only from an integrator (the role of #865) or the human (`admin`); from anyone else they are ignored, not refused. Ignoring, not refusing, because a refusal would be a new gateway rule on `post` for words that today are plain messages, `merged:` must stay postable by anyone (item 84), and a fold that ignores them cannot be walked around by spelling. The role is looked up when the board is built, for each distinct sender of a state word (an internal policy read, one per sender), so removing the role stops its old words from counting; that retroactive effect is intended (a demoted integrator should not keep a queue state) and the cost is that the state of a PR can change when a role is removed.

Fold (`merge_queue` in `domain/merge_queue.rs`). In order of message number, per PR: `ready:` creates a waiting entry (its position is its place among the active entries by enqueue order), or does nothing for an active or merged entry, or, for a dropped one, removes it and enqueues it again at the back. `integrating:` and `checking:` move an active entry to that state (it may go from checking back to integrating after a new head), `dropped:` ends an active entry with its reason, `merged:` from an integrator ends any entry as merged. A state word for a PR nobody enqueued is ignored. `merged:` from a plain member keeps its item 84 meaning (the PR is over) by taking the entry out of the queue without showing it as merged, so a queue never keeps a PR that someone said was over; the integrator's own `merged:` is the only one that shows. A merged PR does not come back by `ready:`. The board shows the active entries and the five most recently ended ones.

Where it shows. A `queue (merge:main held by ivy):` section on `klatch board` (the heading says `merge:main free` when nobody holds the claim), only when there are entries, bounded like the other sections: `1. #7 integrating (ready by alice 5m ago, owner bob)`, `2. #8 waiting (...)`, `#9 dropped: code conflict with main (3m ago; `ready: #9` enqueues it again)`, `#6 merged (10m ago)`. The owner is the holder of a live `pr:N` claim. The board is the only surface: the open-PR section has no command of its own, no wire method and no MCP tool, so the queue adds none, and the member card and the session digest are unchanged (a PR in the queue is already an open PR there). The five words are rows in CONVENTIONS.md and `CONVENTION_WORDS`.

What this does not do. (1) Nothing here verifies a state: a PR shown as merged was said to be merged by the integrator, as item 84's limits say. (2) A queue word can leave the fold if it is archived (#732 applies to these words too). (3) A dropped PR is enqueued again by any member's `ready:`, which an integrator that dropped it for a conflict may drop again; the cost is a line of noise, not a merge. (4) The position is by enqueue order only; there is no priority.

Tests: `domain/merge_queue/tests.rs` (enqueue order, idempotent ready, each transition, plain member state words ignored, a plain member's `merged:`, a dropped PR with its reason and a re-enqueue, the fixed reasons, ended entries bounded, order independent), `gateway/tests/merge_queue.rs` (the board through the gateway, a role removed, the section text with the claim holder and the owner, no section when empty, words deep in a busy room) and bench scenario `ready_words_build_a_merge_queue_on_the_board_that_only_the_integrator_moves`. Tests were written first; the domain tests failed against an empty fold, and the gateway tests failed to compile before the board gained the queue.
