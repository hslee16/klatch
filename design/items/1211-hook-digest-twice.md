The dm nudge the hook digest showed is shown again by wait or read: decided: keep it and document it (#1211, epic #1183; option C; no code, no schema change; 2026-11-24).
What the ticket asks, read from its body (it has no comments): after a hook digest showed a member a message that completes a long two-member exchange, the digest prints the dm nudge (#1190). The hook leaves the read cursor alone, so `klatch wait` or `klatch read` hands the same messages over again, and the client-side dm help of #1209 (`dm_help_lines` in `commands/wait.rs`, used by `read` in `commands/capped.rs` and by `wait`) computes the nudge from what it just handed, so the same crossing message makes the nudge print a second time. The other order is exact: wait or read first moves a cursor, and the hook then finds nothing. The ticket names two fixes: expose the hook position through an operation (a wire change), or have the commands skip the nudge for messages at or below it. It does not ask that a message the digest showed stop being handed by wait or read, and the tests of this repository pin the opposite on purpose: a hook shows each message once and leaves the read cursor alone, so a message the hook cut, muted out or only injected stays readable (comments of `digest_messages` and `hook_read`).

    Which path hands the message again. `do_hook_read` (store) reads past `max(last_seq, hook_seq)`, minus what a capped read showed out of order (`read_shown`, #1405), and moves only `hook_seq`. `do_read` and `poll_past` read past `last_seq` (and `wait_seq`, `wake_seq`), never `hook_seq`, which appears nowhere else but the hook, the idle check, the receipts (`taken_of`) and `member_cursors`. So a message at or below `hook_seq` and above the read cursor is handed again by design, once, with its text, and only the nudge repeats. The reply hint on a dm room is a fixed hint that is true each time and is not the subject of the ticket.

    Why the ticket's smallest step is not safe as it stands. `hook_seq` is where the hook looked, not what it printed. `do_hook_read` moves it over every message found, and the digest then cuts by the output cap: `plan_rooms` keeps a room's other lines (the nudge is one) inside the shared byte budget, drops what does not fit with a count, and collapses rooms that do not fit into one line; at a spent session budget (#1406) fewer lines still print. A message the digest only counted ("N more messages not shown") is at or below `hook_seq` but was not shown. The nudge is computed before rendering (`long_exchange` over all the digest's messages) and is printed only if its unit fits. So "skip the nudge in wait or read for messages at or below `hook_seq`" would, in the rare capped digest, remove the only copy of a nudge the member never saw. No message is lost by that (wait and read still hand every message; only an advisory line is skipped), but it breaks the rule of the cursors that nothing counts as shown that was not shown, and it needs a new operation (the commands cannot see `hook_seq`; `receipt` refuses the reader on purpose and says "never a hook shown-mark").

    What would change under each option. Behaviour, text and storage are stated for the owner to choose; nothing is built. (A) Skip the nudge at or below `hook_seq`: one new read-only call on the transport (`hook_mark(room)`, the caller's own mark, guarded like `read`, listed in the zero trust and authz tables, `PROTOCOL.md` and the method list), the nudge line skipped in `dm_help_lines` when the crossing seq is at or below it; no new text, no schema change; accepted limit: a nudge cut by the cap is not shown by wait or read either. (B) Make "shown" exact: a new `hook` channel in the existing `read_shown` table (channel is free text, so no migration), written by the hook after it printed (a new post-print call from the hook command with the seqs and the nudge crossing it really printed), and read by the same transport call as A; exact under the cap, but the hook gains a second round trip after printing and a failed print must not mark anything. (C) Keep the behaviour and say it: the nudge may appear once in the hook and once in wait or read for the same crossing message, because the line is a short fixed template and a second sight costs a line, not a message; no code, a sentence in `INTEGRATION.md`. (D) Treat a message the hook printed in full as seen by wait and read (not handed again): the largest change, it contradicts the design above (muted-out, cut and merely injected messages stay readable), so it is outside the smallest step and is not proposed.

    The decision (owner, 2026-11-24): option C. The behaviour is kept and documented: the hook leaves the read cursor alone by design, so `wait` and `read` hand a hook-shown message again, and only the dm nudge line can repeat, once in the hook digest and once in `wait` or `read`. The sentence is in `INTEGRATION.md` ("The hook digest and `wait` or `read`"); `CONVENTIONS.md` was not touched. Options A and B stay recorded as the alternatives. They were not chosen because the repeat is a short fixed line, and because A treats `hook_seq` as shown when it is only where the hook looked, not what it printed (the output cap can cut the nudge line or collapse the room after `hook_seq` moved), while B costs a second round trip after every print. If the repeat is later judged worth removing, B is the exact route, with the tests listed in the options above. No message is skipped or lost by any option. Changed: documentation only; no code, text of the digest, schema, wire or golden.
