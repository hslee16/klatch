Failure recovery is a scored scenario of the coordination bench (#1371; no schema change; 2026-11-23). Scenario S6 of experiments/coordbench kills one of three agents with SIGKILL while it holds a claim (K arms) or a board line marked working (B1), has edited its module without committing it, and has had a colleague's question reach it unanswered. The harness evaluates the trigger from the victim's own stream events and the origin's git history (the scenario option `kill_agent`: `after` conditions, each limited to some arms, and a turn fallback), never from a clock, so the kill point is reproducible; the process group is killed, so no hook runs and nothing is released, which is what a crashed or OOM-killed session does (SessionEnd cleanup of #1314 cannot help here). The survivors are not told. A hidden checker scores the final main: four modules (0 to 4), the victim's module finished by a survivor, the orphaned question answered, no duplicate or foreign work, merge state.

    What it measures: recovery rate; seconds and survivor turns from the kill to the first survivor action on the victim's work, and to the commit that completes the victim's module (replayed against the checker) and to the answer; false alarms (a survivor taking over a live agent's module); duplicated recovery; and for K arms how the survivors noticed, read from klatch's own output in their streams: the stuck `dead_holder` and `stale_ask` events (#1182), the mediator's process-exited proposal, the digest line for an ask whose claim lapsed, and a claim of the victim's path succeeding after its lease ran out (the expired and takeover claim events of #1386, seen from the stream). A refused claim and a stale board line are recorded as sightings, not notices.

    Not built: K3 (a supervisor restarts the victim) waits for the role launcher of #1421; the store's claim and stuck events are not exported by the harness yet, so the streams carry only what a survivor was shown; there is no real run until the human approves the M0 budget (#1380). Refs #1371.
