The staged formula may be ahead of the tap by exactly the klatch-server lines before a release, and never after (#1287; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
`release/compare-formula.sh` takes one named option, `--allow-klatch-server`. With it, the staged formula (the release candidate) may differ from the tap formula (the previous release) by exactly two lines besides the version, URLs and hashes: `bin.install "klatch", "klatch-server"` in place of `bin.install "klatch"`, and the one test line `assert_match "usage: klatch-server", shell_output("#{bin}/klatch-server --help")`. Each may be undone once, as an exact line; any other extra, changed or duplicated line is still drift, and a tap that is ahead of the staged copy is drift. `release/bump-formula.sh` passes the option, because on release day the tap formula (0.1.0-dev.2) predates klatch-server and the bump is what adds the lines to it.

`release/verify-published.sh` does not pass the option: after the release the published tap formula and the staged copy (which the bump replaced with the bumped formula) must be equal apart from the version, URLs and hashes, so a tap that lacks the klatch-server lines fails `tap-drift`. The allowance is therefore a before-release rule only, in the one script that writes the lines, and the comparison with no allowance is the proof that the tap really carries them.
