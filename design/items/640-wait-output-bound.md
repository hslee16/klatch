A wait prints at most 20 messages per room, the addressed ones first (#640; no schema change; 2026-11-19).
Left over from item 73: `klatch wait` printed every message that arrived, so a busy lobby could flood a waiting session. It now applies the hook digest cap (`DIGEST_MAX_MESSAGES`, 20 per room): the newest messages that mention the caller come first, then the newest others, printed oldest first as they are read, followed by one line that counts the rest and points at `klatch history ROOM`. The wait still marks everything it returned as read (the cursor is a sequence, so a gap cannot be left), and history keeps the omitted ones. Counting `@all` as addressed, in the wait and in the hook digest, waits on the broadcast decision (#674: a message kind instead of the word), so only a mention of the caller is addressed here.
