The room grant check and write are one store transaction (#897; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
Granting room clearance read the grantor, cap, room label and the grantor's clearance and then wrote the grant as separate steps, so a human's `grantor --off`, cap or label change could land in between and leave a grant row that outlived the grantor it was checked for. `PolicyStore` has a new `atomically` method (a `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction on SQLite, an error on a store that keeps none); `grant_room_clearance` runs the grantor rules, the grant write and the audit row inside it, so the human's change lands before it (the grant is refused) or after it. Statements inside the transaction join it rather than opening their own. A refusal row is written after the transaction ends. The test races a second connection against the write and was red before the change.
