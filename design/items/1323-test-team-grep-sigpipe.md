The team launcher test no longer fails intermittently on a SIGPIPE from `grep -q` (#1323; no schema change; 2026-11-22).
proto/scripts/test-team.sh ran under `set -o pipefail` and tested text with `printf '%s' "$text" | grep -qF want`. grep -q exits at the first match, so under load printf could still be writing, die of SIGPIPE, and fail the pipeline although the text held the phrase (the relaunch prompt check was the one that showed it). The checks now feed grep with a here-string, as verify-published.sh and test-rollback-rehearsal.sh already do. The product script (proto/scripts/team) is unchanged.
