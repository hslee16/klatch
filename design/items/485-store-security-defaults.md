The security-relevant Store defaults are errors, not silent success (#485; no schema change).
The area traits gave about fifty methods a default of `Ok(())` or empty, so a second backend could skip a feature and still run. For the ones whose silence turns a check into a pass, the default is now an error naming what the store does not keep (`area::unsupported`): policy (labels, clearances, roles, floors, tripwires), the audit and the refusals (the breaker's input), blocks and member removal, room pause and close state, deleting rooms, and the post times the rate limit reads. `set_label_keys` has no default, so a store cannot ignore the keys of sealed labels. The SQLite store implements every one and does not change. Left as defaults on purpose: presence, status, scope, parent, mode, hook runs, park, mute, claim notes, anchors, asks and the review queue's readers (a missing feature there loses convenience, not a decision; `enqueue_review` and `issue_credential` already error, and a store with no credentials verifies none). Test stores that relied on the silence now say what they do (`security_defaults_tests` pins the errors on the second-backend spike).
