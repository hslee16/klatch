A Turso backend was tried behind the Store trait and not adopted (#620; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
`experiments/turso-spike/` is a standalone crate (own workspace and lockfile, not built by CI, nothing in `proto/` depends on it) with a `TursoStore` over Turso Database 0.9.0-pre.6. It runs klatch's own conformance suite by path, unchanged: 65 of 108 behaviours pass, and the 43 that fail are areas the spike did not port, not something Turso cannot do. With Turso's native page encryption (aegis256, key from klatch key custody) the file and WAL show no plaintext, a wrong key is refused, and neither engine opens the other's encrypted file. Several processes on one file fail at open unless the experimental multi-process WAL flag is on; with it, 16 processes posting 4800 messages stored every one with dense seq. Speed is comparable to the SQLCipher store.

The decision stands (2026-11-15): stay on SQLite, SQLCipher and OpenSSL. The standalone placement is itself a finding: `cargo deny` with the repository policy fails on the Turso tree because `turso_core` pulls `chrono`, flagged unmaintained (RUSTSEC-2026-0543), and the policy checks all features. Migration through the trait works for messages but loses message ids, because the trait has no way to import a message with its id. Revisit when Turso's encryption leaves experimental, multi-process WAL is supported and `chrono` is gone from `turso_core`, or at the pre-1.0 review. The numbers and the rerun steps are in the research note for #620.
