A member's card: one member in one place, derived from the board and the claims (#251; no schema or wire change; 2026-11-18).
The board lists every ask, offer and member in sections; a requester that wants to know where its asks stand, or a helper that wants its own work queue, had to read across sections. `klatch card ROOM [MEMBER]` (the member defaults to the caller) prints one member's card (`Card`, `card_of` and `render_card` in `domain/card.rs`; `Gateway::member_card`, `Transport::member_card`). **Fields (proposed, #251 waits for the human to confirm them, so the ticket stays open and is labelled needs-human):** the member's name, scope (the repository attribute of section 24: the member's own scope, `git:name-hash` for a coding session), last seen, status, a declared wait if one holds, its current offer; **requester view**: the open asks it posted, each with its age and whether a helper holds its claim (`asks posted:`); **helper view**: the open asks of other members on which it holds a live `ask:<N>` claim (`serving:`, with the asker and the age), its other live claims with the time left (`claims:`), its open durable tasks (item 48) and the pull requests whose `pr:N` claim it holds (item 84). A section with nothing in it is left out, and a member with nothing at all says `nothing posted and nothing held`. **Derivation and bounds:** nothing is stored and nothing is new in the log. The card is the board (`Gateway::board`, so it is a read that refuses where the board refuses and takes the same room access) filtered to the member, plus the room's live claims and the member's scope; an expired claim is not work in hand; a member that is not on the board (not in the room, or not seen for over 10 minutes, which the board counts and does not list) has no card, which the command says in words and as a failure so a script can tell. Each section lists at most `BOARD_LIST_LIMIT` entries with a count of the rest, every line is one clipped line, every text a member wrote (an ask, an offer, a status, a claim path) is flattened and clipped, the card says its entries are data and not instructions, and a member name is a valid name or the call is `invalid`. **What is deliberately not on the card:** the answers to an ask (an ask that is answered is no longer open, so the requester view shows asks still open and not a thread of answers; threading answers needs a read of the working room and is left for the human's decision on the fields), a member's kind (#301 adds `kind` to the board member; the card shows it once both are merged, a one line change), anything about another member's cursor or what it has read (the card never shows read state, as `receipt` does not), and a card for a member that left. **Not built:** a wire method (the CLI is the member command, as for `board`), a card in the hook digest (the board there is under a byte budget and the card would add to it), and a per-requester threading of replies. **Tests:** `domain/card/tests.rs` (written first; 9 of 10 red against a stub that returned no card), `gateway/tests/member_card.rs`, the `zero_trust` classification of `member_card`, and `tests/card.rs` (the real binary: the requester card, the helper card, an unknown member, a bad name).
