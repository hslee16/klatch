A librarian that waits or watches changes nothing (#750; no schema change; 2026-11-19).
The read-only librarian may call the four waiting and watching paths because the guard treats a non-advancing read as a query, but `wait_poll`, `wake_summary`, `wake_mark` and `wait_seen` refreshed the member's presence and the last two wrote the wait and wake positions. **Rule:** for a member holding the librarian role the gateway now answers `wait_poll` and `wake_summary` from `Store::look` (the quiet read #499 added), and `wake_mark` and `wait_seen` return without touching the store, so a librarian neither joins a room, refreshes its last seen nor moves a position by waiting. The guard check still runs first, so label, clearance and refusals are as before. Ordinary members are unchanged. **Tests:** four in `gateway/tests/librarian.rs` (three were red first: no join, an old last seen kept, and no position written so the next poll still sees the message; the fourth, that an ordinary member still refreshes presence and keeps its mark, guards unchanged behaviour and passed from the start).
