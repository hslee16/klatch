The room creator is the initial grantor of room-scoped clearance, within strict caps (#893, decision on #887; part of #180; no schema change; 2026-11-18).
Clearance was global per member and a floor act. A room now has a grantor (`grantor:<room>`, the creator when the room is made by a join or a post, replaced or removed only by the human) who may give one member a clearance in that room only (`room-clear:<room>:<member>`), through `klatch grant ROOM MEMBER LABEL`. Both are keys of the existing policy table, so there is no schema change and no migration.

A grantor can give only what it holds. The grant is refused with its own rule unless: the caller is the room's grantor now (`grantor`); the grantee is not the caller (`self-grant`); the label is not the top of the chain (`top-level`); it is at most the agent cap (`agent-cap`), the room's label (`room-label`) and the caller's own global clearance (`own-clearance`). Room grants are not passed on: a grantor that is in a room only by a grant holds, as a grantor, its global clearance. The decision reads the policy table and roles, never a message; a message that says "grant bob private" does nothing. A grant is audited (`grant-clearance`: grantor, grantee, room, label) and a refusal keeps a refusal row.

The human controls it as floor acts, refused to every agent and to the admin agent: `klatch admin grantor ROOM MEMBER [--off]` names or removes the grantor, `klatch admin room-clear ROOM MEMBER LABEL|--off` sets or revokes a grant. A grant is read back only while it is a known label at most the current agent cap and not the top level, so lowering the cap or replacing the chain turns an old grant off. A room made again under a name that once existed starts with its creator as grantor and none of the old grants, and deleting a run's rooms deletes their grantor and grants.

Where a global clearance check learned about room grants: `check_clearance` (every room operation through `authorize`, and the reviewer's queue) and the invite check (`check_invitee_clearance`) now read the member's clearance in that room (the higher of its global clearance and its grant). Left on the global clearance on purpose: a direct-message room's label (the lower of the two members' global clearances), the label keys a process is given (`sealed_labels_within`, #896), and a grantor's own ceiling. Not done: a wire method for granting (#895) and a single-transaction check and write (#897).
