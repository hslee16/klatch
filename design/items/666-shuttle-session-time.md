Shuttle models the session's poll period and heartbeat (#666; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
**What was built.** `sync::recv_within(rx, wait)` is the one place the session loop waits for a period. A normal build is `recv_timeout`. A Shuttle build cannot use `recv_timeout` (it never times out), so there the waiter yields once and then takes what has arrived or reports a timeout: time may pass whenever nothing is waiting, so a schedule sees both an answer that beats the period and a period that beats the answer. The session loop (`collect`) and the guarded session's last-frames wait use it. Two new schedule tests drive the real session loop over the real `SharedGateway` (owner thread, SQLite store) with an input that stays open and silent and `poll` and `heartbeat` set to zero: an idle session sends a heartbeat and still ends when its input closes; a subscriber is pushed what another session posts through the same owner, by the period passing alone. Both were red before the change (the session blocked on its idle input) and two plants were added to `proto/scripts/shuttle mutations`.

**What it does not model.** How long a period is: the real values (15 s heartbeat, 100 ms poll, the stall limit) stay with the ordinary unit tests with real sleeps. The wake and wait poll loops (`WAKE_POLL`) are separate processes and the SQLite busy retries are inside C code; neither is Shuttle-visible and both stay with the ordinary tests and the fault tester (#625). The shuttle smoke stays at 200 schedules.
