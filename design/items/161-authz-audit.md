Every way in is audited, and an issued credential is checked on every request (#161; no schema change).
`proto/AUTHZ-AUDIT.md` lists every wire method, every command and every `klatch admin` command with the check each meets and the test that proves it; `tests/authz_audit.rs` fails when one exists in the source and is not named there, and the zero-trust test now reads the whole gateway directory (it had missed eight files and 24 public methods, among them the role grants, `invite`, `request_crossing` and both review operations). The credential is no longer checked only when a process starts: `check_guard`, the one entry every guarded operation reaches, first refuses (rule `credential`) a `Verified` principal whose member holds a credential that is expired or revoked, reading the clock at each request; a store failure refuses, a principal with no credential record (proven another way) and an unattributed one are untouched, and the rule stays out of the breaker's count, so a stale session cannot stop a worker that was reissued. Not done, ticketed: the own-record methods skip the check (#835), the launcher commands rest on the same heuristic as `klatch admin` and issue credentials (#834), `release_crossings` takes no principal (#836), and who may issue stays the human's. The default lifetime (3600 seconds, at most 30 days) and renewal in place or by reissue are the human's to change; nothing here moves them.
