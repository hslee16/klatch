An unclassified public method still fails `every_public_method_of_the_gateway_is_classified` naming the method (shown with a temporary method), a listed name that is not in the source still fails, and the same test now also fails when a method is listed twice (in `GUARDED` and `OWN_RECORD`, or in two areas).
