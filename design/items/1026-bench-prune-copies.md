Old content-named bench binary copies are pruned under the build lock (#1026; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
The `klatch-<hash>` copies in `target/bench-bin` (#752) never changed after being made, so every rebuild with new content left another binary behind. `obtain_binary` now prunes under the same `.build.lock`, after the copy is in place: it keeps the newest three `klatch-*` files by modification time and always keeps the current one, even if it is the oldest. Pruning is best effort: a listing or removal error is logged to stderr and the build still succeeds. Tests use a temp directory, the fake builder, and an injected remover that fails.
