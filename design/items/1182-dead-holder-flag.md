A claim whose holder's process is gone is flagged as a dead_holder stuck event (#1182, flag only; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
A helper killed after claiming left its ask claimed until the claim lapsed (about 90 s), and `klatch stuck` showed only `stale_ask`. **The event:** `klatch stuck ROOM` and the wire method `stuck` gain a sixth kind, `dead_holder` (`room`, `claim`, `holder`; key `dead_holder:ROOM:CLAIM`; `claim` is `ask:N` for an ask). It is derived on every call and stored nowhere, with the same guard as the other kinds. A live claim is flagged when its holder has a session record and the liveness probe of #790 (`LeaseSource`, the same one behind `process_exited`) says `Gone`; every other verdict, a record the probe cannot read, a holder with no session record (a bot) and a gateway with no lease source flag nothing, because unknown counts as alive. A lapsed claim is not flagged. `claim` is the key itself when it is plain (letters, digits, `_ . / : # @ + -`, at most 80 characters) and the word `(unnamed)` otherwise, so a claim path a member chose cannot carry text into a line or a post. **Flag only:** nothing is released, shortened or extended; the existing lease rules (#304) are untouched and the claim lapses by its own time to live. **The line:** the mediator proposes with one fixed template from the event's fields, `mediator proposal: @HOLDER holds CLAIM in ROOM but its process has exited. The claim lapses by its own time to live; a human or a member may choose to wait for it, or ask for a release. A proposal only: ...`; the holder name is validated like every mediator line and a holder that is the mediator is skipped. **Decision left to the human:** releasing the claim when the holder is provably gone is not built. Options: (1) flag only, as now; (2) release at once when the probe says `Gone`, which frees the ask for a second helper without waiting out the TTL but acts on a probe that can be wrong across machines or after a session id is reused; (3) release after a grace period (the lease rules already shorten a gone holder's claims by `lease:grace`, 300 s by default, when someone asks); (4) a human `klatch admin` release only. **Not covered:** a holder on another machine (the probe says unknown), and the stuck-cases fixture corpus, which has no process table to fake.
