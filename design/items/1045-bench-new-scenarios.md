The bench gains ten functional scenarios for features merged since its last extension (#1045; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
The standing rule is that the bench is expanded after each batch of features. The scenarios live in three new files under `proto/klatch-bench/src/scenarios/` and drive the real binary through `World`, each with origin tags naming its tickets: `ask_commands.rs` (one-call `ask` and `answer` and the lost race, #241; `find`, read only, #239; the asker's `reopen #N:`, #232), `member_state.rs` (done status clears on a post or claim, shows its age, and `available` stays, #260; `who` mode and wake path, #677; `klatch grant`, #893 and #897; a member rate limit over the global one and `admin rate-limit --list`, #972 and #1023) and `crossing_view.rs` (`klatch crossings` and the wire `my_crossings` with the once-only waiting line in the requester's digest, #516, #679, #961; a librarian that waits joins nothing and moves no place, #750; `history --last N` filled past planted canary rows, #1008).

Already covered, so no new scenario: the receipt shown and read states (#675) are asserted in `receipts.rs`, and the unjoined target refusal of `cross` (#679) in `judge.rs`.

Not exercisable through the CLI in a bench world, so skipped: the advisory mark on read, wait, digest, tail and history (#878, #990, #1032), because a flag-mode room requirement needs a harness the gateway verified through a live process and its registry; own-record refusal after a credential ends (#835), the `release_crossings` audit naming the releaser (#836) and the `klatch worker list` cap and last renewal (#948, #1007), because each needs an issued credential, and the human alone issues credentials.

Evidence that the scenarios can fail: three product behaviours were changed one at a time on a scratch copy and the matching scenario went red each time, then the change was reverted. Letting any sender's `reopen` count made the reopen scenario fail; making `clear_done` match nothing made the status scenario fail; making every mode get a nudge made the who-mode scenario fail.

Timing metrics: none of the new scenarios reports a metric of its own beyond the runner's `integrity_ms`, so no new baseline entry is needed. CI does not run the bench (only the two test jobs run on a pull request); run it by hand from `proto/` with `cargo run -q -p klatch-bench --locked -- --budgets klatch-bench/baseline.json`.
