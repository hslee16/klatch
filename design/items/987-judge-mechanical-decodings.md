The default rules judge undoes mechanical wrapping of a credential, and nothing more (#987; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
`credential_kind` (proto/klatch/src/domain/credentials.rs), which the rules judge and the gateway storage check both use, first matches the text as it stands, then tries these mechanical forms and matches each with the same rules: zero-width and other invisible format characters stripped; a run of equal-width words (`ab cd ef gh`, a value spaced out) joined; a long bare token line glued to the long token that ends the line above (a JWT cut over lines); a long token read as base64 (standard or URL-safe, padding optional) or hex and swapped for its printable decoding in place, so a label in front of it still counts; a long token reversed, matched by well known shape only (never by label, since the words around it are not reversed); JSON, YAML and env field names that hold a secret (`refresh_token`, `access_token`, `id_token`, `auth_token`, `session_token`, `bearer_token`, `client_secret`, `secret_key`) followed by a long value with a digit that is not a placeholder; and a value read out one character at a time (twelve or more single characters with a lower case letter and two of digit, upper case, symbol). Texts over 64 KiB get only the plain match.

The decodings stay mechanical on purpose. A decoded form must be printable text and must then look like a credential by the existing rules, so a PNG header in base64, a hash in hex, `"grant_type":"refresh_token"`, a counted `1 2 3 4 ...` and prose about tokens pass. What the rules judge does not do, by the human decision of 2026-11-21: a passphrase of plain words with no label, and a secret that only the surrounding talk marks as one. No heuristic is added for those because it would risk blocking legitimate crossings. They are left to the opt-in hosted judge (`klatch judge run --hosted-judge`, the owner's own use only; it never runs in tests or in the bench by default). On the hard corpus the one secret the rules judge still lets across is hsec-010, a plain-word passphrase.

Measured with `klatch-bench judge-eval --judge rules`: on corpus-hard.jsonl the secret leaks (secret-credential and code-log-secret items) went from 8 to 1 over both splits (dev 3 to 1, test 5 to 0), total hard leaks from 74 to 72 of 94 (dev) and from 61 to 56 of 72 (test); over-blocking stayed 0 of 66 (dev) and 0 of 38 (test); on the first corpus the figures are unchanged (leaks 41 of 79 dev and 30 of 55 test, over-blocking 0 of 118 and 0 of 79).
