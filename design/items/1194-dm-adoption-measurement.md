Measuring dm adoption with the variance harness (#1194, epic #1183; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
Whether agents move a multi-message exchange to a dm was only ever a one-run, by-hand record (#210, #950). experiments/dm-adoption makes it repeatable: `run.sh` sets up a throwaway store (the KLATCH_HOME and KLATCH_KEY_DIR that experiments/variance/repeat.py gives each run, or its own), runs `klatch init`, and starts two real `claude -p` sessions: alpha asks how to slugify a title and holds four requirements back, helper owns the code and must learn four facts one question at a time. Neither prompt mentions a dm or a second room. `metric.py` then reads the store as alpha with `klatch history` (never an agent's self report) and prints one JSON line in the form repeat.py reads.

    An exchange is a pair of members with 3 or more messages in one room; it moved when the pair has a `dm.` room or an `ask-N` room holding at least `--min-dm` messages (default 3). The `exchange` entry carries `adoption` (moved over exchanges), `exchanges`, `moved`, `lobby_messages`, `dm_messages`, `reply_latency_s` (in dm and ask-N rooms), `lobby_reply_latency_s`, and `outcome` (`no-exchange` when nothing reached 3 messages, which repeat.py counts as a failed run, not a zero); `alpha` and `helper` carry turns, tokens, cost and duration from their `claude -p` results. repeat.py reports the mean of `adoption` over N runs (the adoption share) with its sample sd, min and max, plus the message counts and latency; its spend cap, timeout and failure count apply unchanged. Arms are `--arm baseline|text|nudge|private`: baseline, text and nudge share the prompts and differ only in the klatch binary under test (`KLATCH_BIN`); private tells alpha to open with `klatch dm helper "seeking: ..."` until `klatch ask --to` exists. The tests (`test-dm-adoption.sh`, in the gate) use a canned store, a fake klatch and a fake claude, so they spend nothing; the real-run numbers (model, N, share and spread per arm) are the human's to collect and post on #1194.
