The B1 baseline shares klatch's message prefixes, and real coordbench runs need a budget approval file (#1365, #1399; no schema change; 2026-11-23). The human decided on 2026-11-23 that the B1 and B1-roles agents use the same message prefixes as klatch's convention and the K arms (`seeking:`, `answer:`, `handoff:`, plus `ready:`, `merged:` and `dropped:` in B1-roles), so that the baseline is as strong as it can be without klatch. The K1 and K2 prompts name no ask classes, so B1 does not either, and B0 has no messaging tool and no prefixes. The equal-quality rule stays: `check_parity.py` keeps the coordination sections within 15 percent of each other, now also checks the prefixes (B1 and B1-roles have them, B0 has none, K1 and K2 still say the same words), and still forbids klatch words; the prefixes are plain words and pass.

  The harness refuses real agents without a budget approval file: `--budget-approved-in FILE`, a JSON object made at run time by the coordinator outside the repository (none is committed) with `approved_by`, `approved_on`, `suite_budget_usd`, `per_run_cap_usd`, `scope`, `models` and `expires_on`. `experiments/coordbench/approval.py` validates it: a missing, malformed, expired or future-dated file, a model not listed, or a command-line cap above the file's refuses with exit 2 and writes nothing; caps not given take the file's numbers and caps given below them stand. The ledger header and `suite.json` record the approver, date and scope. Tested with the fake driver only.
