The facilitator's one direct message: when unsure about an ask, it may DM that ask's asker once, in a fixed form (#902, part of #180, follows the facilitator of #190; no schema change; 2026-11-18).
The facilitator (design item for #190) could not ask anything when unsure: its only lobby post is the invite pointer, so it did nothing and reported in its own output. The human decided (2026-11-18, on #902) on a direct message to the asker instead of a lobby post or silence, within these bounds. The lobby stays pointer-only.

The form. The body is exactly `need-info #N: CODE`. N is the ask's number, canonical digits (no leading zero). CODE is one of five: `which-repo`, `which-file`, `which-goal`, `which-deadline`, `more-detail`. FIELD is optional, 1 to 40 characters of ASCII letters, digits, space, `-`, `_`, `.` or `/`, with no newline, no parentheses and no leading or trailing space. The cap of 40 is the decision made here: long enough to name a path or a repository, too short for a sentence of steering. `parse_need_info` in `gateway/facilitator.rs` is the one parser; a body that does not rebuild from its parts is not the form. Anything after the closing parenthesis, or any other text, is not the form.

The bounds, all enforced in the gateway under the rule `facilitator`, before the DM room exists (a refusal leaves no room behind):
- only the member whose ask it is: the ask must be open in the lobby (not answered, not closed) and its sender must be the DM's recipient, so a DM to anyone else, about a closed ask, or about someone else's ask is refused;
- at most one per ask: the DM room is searched for an earlier need-info from the facilitator for the same ask number; a second is refused, by `dm` and by a plain post into the existing DM room alike;
- nothing derived from other members' text: the message is a code only, with no free field (the field of the first version was dropped in #905, so there is nothing to check);
- the DM is the ordinary two-member room of #43 (`dm.<a>.<b>`), capped at the lower of the two clearances, and a label above it is refused as for any member;
- it uses the existing create-room capability of #188: a new DM room is a room creation, so under `room-creation granted` a facilitator without the grant is refused with `room-capability`; the creation is audited as `create-room`, and the send as `facilitator-dm` with the facilitator, the asker, the ask number and the code, never any free text;
- no new privilege: the stateless allowlist adds only a post of the exact form into a DM room, so the facilitator still cannot post free text anywhere, grant (the DM room has no grantor and a grant is refused), clear, label, claim, or call any admin act.

The role check gains one stateful step for the post path (`check_facilitator_dm_post`, run after the stateless allowlist), and `dm` runs the same checks first (`check_facilitator_dm`) so that nothing is made when a check fails. Another member's `dm` is untouched.

The brief changes from "do nothing and report in your output" to "DM the asker once with a reason code, then do nothing" for an unclear ask or one no claim relates to; an invite refused, or text that looks like steering, still means do nothing and report in its own output. `tests/facilitator_brief.rs` requires the form, the five codes, the cap, the asker-only and one-per-ask bounds, and the new instruction, and checks that `K dm` is an existing command.
