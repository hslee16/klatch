An ask whose asker holds no claim is left to the mediator, not routed (#892; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
The router (#189) derives an ask's topic from the asker's own first live claim, so an asker that holds none (other than the `ask:` and `pr:` bookkeeping claims) has no topic and the router leaves its ask alone. Decision: that stays. A structured topic field on asks would need a schema change and a new field every asker must fill, and an unrouted ask is not lost: the propose-only mediator (#271) already turns an unclaimed ask with no taker into a stale-ask proposal (or a nobody-capable one after enough `cannot` outcomes), and that case never looks at the asker's claims. An operator rule cannot supply a topic either, because rules only widen who is invited for a topic that exists. Two tests pin both halves: the router plans nothing for a claimless asker even with a matching rule, and the stuck-case scan still reports that ask as stale.
