The room grantor can grant over the wire: `grant_room_clearance` and the three client wrappers (#895, follow-up of #893; part of #180; no schema change; 2026-11-20).
#893 gave the room grantor `klatch grant ROOM MEMBER LABEL` and the gateway call `grant_room_clearance`, but a bot or client speaking the wire protocol could not grant. The wire method `grant_room_clearance` (`room`, `member`, `label`, result `{}`) now calls the same gateway method as the command, so the checks live in the gateway and are the same on every transport: the operation `GrantClearance`, the room's checks, the role, and the rules `grantor`, `self-grant`, `top-level`, `agent-cap`, `room-label` and `own-clearance`. A refusal frame carries the rule; a missing or wrongly typed parameter is `invalid_params`. The grantor is the transport's principal; no param names it.

Wrappers: `grant_room_clearance` in the Python client and the Rust `klatch::client`, `grantRoomClearance` in the TypeScript client; `tests/client_methods.rs` checks each. PROTOCOL.md section 5 lists the method and `proto/AUTHZ-AUDIT.md` has the wire row and the updated `grant` row; the known gap for #895 is closed. Naming or removing a grantor and revoking a grant stay floor acts of the human with no wire method. Still open: #897 (check and write in one transaction) and #896.
