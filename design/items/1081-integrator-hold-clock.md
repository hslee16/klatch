The integrator hold clock restarts when a merge is finished, not when it is posted (#1081; script and test only; no schema change; 2026-11-21).
Gate step 11 failed intermittently because `test-integrator.sh` ("a merged word resets the hold clock") ran the helper with a 1 second hold limit. The clock was reset when the merged word was posted, but the work that finishes a merge (the main fetch, the board read, the mergeable-state reads and, with `--merge-without-update`, the verification of main) ran after that; on a loaded machine it took over a second and the helper released merge:main right after it merged, which is the failure seen. In `proto/scripts/integrator` the clock is now also restarted after that work, so a working integrator is not released for the time its own merge bookkeeping takes. The test gained a `board-sleep` knob in the fake klatch and a 4 second board read against a 3 second limit; it was red before the change with the exact failure text of the gate and is green after.
