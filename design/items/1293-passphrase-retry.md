Choosing a new admin passphrase asks again (#1293; no schema change; 2026-11-22).
`klatch admin init-passphrase` and `reset-passphrase` (and the new passphrase of a change) used to exit on the first too-short or mismatched entry, so the person had to rerun the command. `ask_new` in `klatch::proof` now asks up to `NEW_ATTEMPTS` (3) times in all, and between attempts tells the person why through the new `Prompt::notice` (too short, too long, edge spaces, or the two entries differ; the terminal prompt writes it to stderr, any other prompt says nothing by default). A too-short first entry skips the confirmation and starts the next attempt. After the third bad attempt the command exits with the last reason and nothing was written.

Choosing a new passphrase proves nothing about an old one, so these attempts are never charged to the failure limit. The old passphrase of a change and the recovery code of a reset are each asked once and charged as before; the retries come after that proof and change no count. The entries are still read from the terminal with echo off, never from the environment, arguments, files or pipes, and the notice never holds what was typed.
