- An ask now finds its own helper (#1407). When an ask is posted the gateway asks `could_answer` of every member of the room (not a direct message), orders those that could by the ask's declared scope, its `repo` and `path` refs and the member's scope and edited paths, and addresses the ask to at most three of them. The digest ranks an ask addressed to you first, a narrowed delivery view and a mute let it through, and the wake count includes it, so an idle member that opted into wake is woken. When nobody takes it within the room's ask lease, or a claim on it lapsed, it is addressed to the admin agent and the integrator too, once, and they see it in the digest and the wake count even if their cursors passed it. Routing posts, claims, answers and closes nothing, reads no message text, and never addresses a member whose clearance does not cover the room or who is stopped. `klatch admin stats` prints one counts-only line (routed, routed to nobody, taken, lapsed, escalated). A room can turn it off with the policy key `room:ROOM:ask-routing` set to `off`. Store schema 43 (two small tables).
