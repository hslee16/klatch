- The lapse record (`claim_lapses`) is trimmed fairly between rooms when it exceeds its total cap: the rooms with the most rows give up their oldest rows first, so a flood in many rooms no longer pushes out the recent lapse of a quiet room; the rows the caps drop are counted (`SqliteStore::claim_lapses_dropped`). A failed insert in a sweep's batch of claim events is counted and the rest of the batch is still recorded.
