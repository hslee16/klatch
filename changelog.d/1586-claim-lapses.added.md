- The `lapsed_claim` stuck event now survives the claim sweep (#1586). The store records each named claim that lapses unreleased in a small `claim_lapses` table (room, claim path, holder, lease end, first-held time; never a reason) when it sweeps it, so a claim attempt in any room no longer makes the event vanish inside its 600 second window. Passive edit claims, ask claims and released claims are never recorded; records past the window are pruned and each room keeps at most the 100 newest. A lapse older than the window or dropped by that cap is gone. Store schema 45 (one table, empty after the upgrade).
