- The `facilitator` role (`klatch admin facilitator MEMBER [--off]`): a member that routes asks into topical rooms. It may read, see who and claims, create a room, invite and post the invite pointer, and nothing else; every other call, every admin act and the room grant are refused with the rule `facilitator` before any guard is asked, and a room it creates has no grantor. `proto/scripts/facilitator-brief.md` is the instruction text for a session that holds the role (#190).
