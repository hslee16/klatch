- A worker credential now renews on use (#161). `klatch worker issue --ttl` is an idle timeout (default 3600 seconds): each successful request pushes the expiry out by that long, up to a hard cap from issue that renewal never passes (`--max-life`, default 24 hours, at most 30 days). A revoke still stops the credential at its next request, an idle credential lapses at its timeout, and each renewal is audited as `renew-credential` (at most one row per minute of use). Store schema 31 adds `idle` and `cap` to `credentials`; a credential issued before it is never renewed.
