- A helper that is waiting on an asker is told within seconds when the asker's process has exited (the wait ends with `[klatch wait: asker-exited ...]` and a line naming the asker), instead of after ten minutes of silence. It uses the liveness probe where the asker has a recorded session on the same machine; anything the probe cannot read changes nothing, and the ten minute rule stays as the fallback (#790).
