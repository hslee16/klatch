- `klatch-server https --listen IP:PORT --tls-cert PEM --tls-key PEM --tls-client-ca PEM [--allow-off-loopback]`: the klatch wire (HTTP and server-sent events, MCP at `POST /mcp`) behind a mutual TLS listener (rustls). You supply the certificate, the key (mode 0600 or the server refuses to start) and a CA file for client certificates; a client without a valid certificate from that CA is refused at the handshake, there is no plaintext mode and no way to skip a check, and a certificate gates the port only (the caller is still the worker credential). Loopback unless `--allow-off-loopback` is given (#108, #157). Limits for a peer with no certificate: `--handshake-secs` (default 3, 1 to 60), `--max-handshakes` (default a quarter of `--max-connections`) and `--stall-secs` (default 60, 30 to 86400); out-of-range values refuse to start.
