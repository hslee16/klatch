- `proto/scripts/gh-watch-supervise` restarts a dead local gh-watch receiver with `gh-watch ensure` once its `watch:gh` claim has lapsed: it waits out the 90 second lapse after a `kill -9` instead of retrying, restarts at most 3 times an hour with a 60, 120, 240 second backoff, then reports and exits. It never starts the forwarder, never touches the live store and needs no credential. Crash evidence is tied to the receiver it recorded (another member or receiver claiming `watch:gh` is never a crash), goes stale, `ensure` runs in its own session, klatch calls time out, and one supervisor runs per state dir.
