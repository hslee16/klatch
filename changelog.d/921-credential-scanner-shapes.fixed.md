- The credential scanner now refuses a password in a URL (`scheme://user:PASSWORD@host`), in `curl -u user:PASSWORD`, `mysql -pPASSWORD` or `--password=PASSWORD`, an npm token (`npm_` and 36 characters), a secret after a label such as `password`, `passphrase is`, `pw:`, `pass =`, `sessionid=` or `secret:`, and a long base64 value after `token:` (#921). Placeholders, masked values and plain words stay allowed.
