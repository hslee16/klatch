- `klatch init --stop-warn` (and `klatch consent stop-warn on|off`, `--no-stop-warn`) is a new opt-in consent, a flag on the PostToolUse, SessionEnd and shared Stop hook commands like `--machine-status`. A Stop hook that carries it prints one fixed-template `systemMessage` line for the human when a turn ends with background tasks or crons still running: how many remain, the task kinds (shell, monitor, agent, other; read tolerantly from a `type` or `kind` string, anything else counts as other) and a reminder to stop them or start one wait only. It never echoes a command or description, stops nothing (turning it on adds klatch's Stop hook, with its usual record of background counts and liveness); absent or wrong-typed `background_tasks` and `session_crons` print nothing. `SubagentStop` is not covered (#1594); the real entry shape and the channel a model sees are unverified (#1593) (#1344).
