- `Backend` has a cancel hook (`canceller()` and `handle_cancellable()`, #1562). The default reports "not cancelled" and runs a look to its end; the shared-gateway backend (`SharedBackend`) registers each look under its call id, so a look waiting on the owner thread ends within one 20 ms tick of a cancel with the `Cancelled` outcome, and a look the owner has not started is never run. `klatch acp` wires `session/cancel` to it: a prompt blocked in the backend now ends with `stopReason` `cancelled` and no answer at once, instead of when the look returns. A cancel for another id, after the look finished, or repeated does nothing further, and another connection's look with the same id is never touched (#1562).
