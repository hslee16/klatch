- `klatch-server https`: a reload build that hangs (a file on a stalled mount) no longer disables reload for good. It blocks the next reloads only until it has been stuck for 120 seconds; then the next SIGHUP gives it up, logs that it was abandoned (a restart clears the thread), and reloads, and the abandoned build's late result is never applied. At most three abandoned builds may be stuck at once; after that the log says "restart the server" instead of retrying.
