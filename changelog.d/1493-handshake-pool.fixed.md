- `klatch-server https`: connections that have not finished the TLS handshake no longer take places from the session cap, and one source address (IPv4 /32, IPv6 /64) may hold only `--handshakes-per-source` of the handshake pool (default 2, at most `--max-handshakes`); a connection over a limit is closed without a byte. A client that sends one byte at a time is now cut at `--handshake-secs` (the deadline was not checked between reads). Refusals are logged as one counted line every ten seconds, with no address (#1493).
