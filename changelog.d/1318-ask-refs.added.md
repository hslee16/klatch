- An ask can carry references to artifacts outside klatch and to other klatch objects (`klatch ask ROOM TEXT --ref KIND:VALUE`, repeatable, with `--ref-label` and `--ref-snippet-file`; the wire `post` param `refs`; and the Rust, TypeScript and Python clients). A ref has an open-ended kind (`ticket`, `pr`, `commit`, `path`, `url`, `tool`, `message`, `room` and any other token, with known kinds checked and unknown kinds kept as text), a value, an optional label and an optional snippet of up to 8192 bytes; up to 32 per ask, refused and never cut when over a cap. klatch never fetches, opens, runs or resolves one, refuses credentials in them and shows them as inert text in `asks`, the board, `read`, `wait`, `could-answer` and the stream, and `klatch find` matches them. A dm ask carries no refs. The store schema is now 38 (#1318).
