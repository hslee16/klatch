- `proto/scripts/integrator --merge-without-update` (off by default; `--verify-gate` and `--verify-bench` are optional): merges a queued PR whose merge-tree against current main is clean without updating its branch, once the checks of its current head are green, then verifies main from the check events of the merge commit and, if main is red because of that merge, reverts it through a branch `revert-N` and a PR (never a direct update of main), posts the fixed words `reverted: #N` or `halted: main red after #N` (exit 5), and never reverts twice (#867). This replaces the up-to-date rule for those PRs and is the human's decision to turn on. See `proto/scripts/INTEGRATOR.md`.
