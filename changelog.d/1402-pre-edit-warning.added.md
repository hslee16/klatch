- `klatch init --pre-edit-warn` (opt in, off by default; `klatch consent pre-edit-warn on`) adds a PreToolUse hook of its own for Edit, Write, MultiEdit and NotebookEdit that prints one bounded line before the edit when another member holds a live claim, explicit or passive, on the path (symlinks, `..` spellings and files that do not exist yet are judged by where they land). It shows names and counts only, never a claim reason or message text, marks the names as data, prints nothing when there is nothing to say, never blocks (`--enforce` stays the blocking hook and is untouched), never reads or consumes a message, and fails open with the usual visible line when the store cannot be read (#1402).
- `klatch init --pre-edit-claims` (opt in; needs `--passive-claims`) makes the same hook take the passive claim before the first edit of a path, so first edits are covered at edit time; `--no-passive-claims` drops it and keeps the warning. `klatch doctor` reports the pre-edit hook (#1458).
