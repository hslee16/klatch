- The review queue gains two partial indexes on its waiting items (store schema 46, additive, applied on open): the dedupe lookup and per-requester count of every crossing request, and the page reads of the waiting queue, no longer scan the whole table with its resolved history (#1744).
- The conformance probe for stores that declare reviews now also runs the cursor-paging and per-requester count test, and the test double has faults that the paging, count and capped-enqueue tests must catch; the default `enqueue_review_capped` documents its fixed order (dedupe by requester and target room, then the cap) and that it is not atomic, so a multi-writer store must override it as SQLite does (#1744, #1747).
