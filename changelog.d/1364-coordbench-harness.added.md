- The coordination bench has a harness, a results ledger and a report generator (`experiments/coordbench/run.py`, `report.py`, `compare.py`, standard library Python only): it runs a scenario against the arms B0, B1, K1 and K2 N times each in isolated temp stores with fresh fixture checkouts and per-run bare origins, through a fake (scripted, no model) or a headless `claude -p` agent driver, with per-run and per-suite spend caps, timeouts and a dry run; keeps every run as a JSON record in the SPEC schema; and generates REPORT.md with Fisher exact, Mann-Whitney, bootstrap intervals, Holm correction, an exploratory label under N of 5 and a regression comparison with a previous release. Real agents stay off until the human approves a budget (#1364, #1362).
